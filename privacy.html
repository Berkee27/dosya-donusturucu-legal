Privacy Policy

Dosya Dönüştürücü / File Converter
Package: com.berke.dosyadonusturucu
Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dosya Dönüştürücü / File Converter (“the App”, “we”, “us”) accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

1. Data We Process

  • Account and authentication information: account identifier, email address when provided by an authentication provider, and authentication/provider information required to sign in and maintain your account.
  • Purchase and entitlement information: product identifiers, subscription or lifetime entitlement status, purchase/restore status, and related transaction metadata supplied by Google Play, Apple App Store, and/or RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • App/device anti-abuse information: an app-scoped device identifier may be sent securely to our backend so that a pseudonymous device key can be derived for Premium trial abuse prevention. The raw identifier is not intended to be retained as a stored server-side identifier.
  • Files you choose: documents, images, PDFs, spreadsheets, structured-data files, and other files that you explicitly select for conversion, editing, previewing, saving, opening, or sharing.
  • Local app history and recent-file metadata: information such as file name, file type, timestamps, operation type, and local file references used to provide History and Recent Files features.

2. How Files Are Processed

The App’s current conversion and editing functions are designed to process user-selected files on the device. File contents are not uploaded to our application backend for the purpose of performing those conversions or edits.

If you choose an operating-system action such as Share, Open With, or another third-party app/service, the destination you select may receive the file. That transfer is initiated by you and is governed by the destination provider’s privacy practices.

3. How We Use Information

  • provide sign-in and account functionality;
  • provide file conversion, editing, history, recent-file, and related app features;
  • verify Premium purchases and restore entitlements;
  • enforce the limited free Premium trial and prevent repeated trial abuse;
  • maintain security, prevent fraud or misuse, and troubleshoot service problems;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Service Providers and Data Sharing

  • Supabase — authentication, account-related backend functions, and server-side Premium trial quota records.
  • RevenueCat — purchase entitlement and subscription/lifetime-access status management.
  • Google Play — Android billing, purchase processing, and Google authentication where used.
  • Apple — Apple authentication and App Store purchase processing where applicable.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

5. Premium Trial and Device Identifier

The App provides a limited number of free Premium uses. To prevent the trial from being repeatedly reset through account switching, app restarts, or reinstallation, an app-scoped device identifier may be used to derive a pseudonymous server-side device key. Trial state is evaluated using both account-level and device-level records.

This information is used for security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention, not for advertising.

6. Advertising

The current version of the App does not display third-party advertising and does not use the Premium plan as an “ad-free” upgrade.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data, including encrypted network transport (HTTPS/TLS) for backend communications, access controls, and separation between application data and payment processing handled by app-store providers.

8. Data Retention

Account-related information is generally retained while your account remains active and as needed to provide the service. Local file/history information remains on your device until removed by you, removed through app functionality, cleared with app data, or otherwise removed by the operating system.

When an account is deleted, account-associated data under our control is deleted or anonymized as appropriate. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud/abuse prevention, legal compliance, financial recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or enforcement of purchase/trial rules. Any retained information is limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you have an App account, you may request deletion of your account and associated data using the in-app account settings where available and through our public account-deletion page:

Account Deletion

Deleting your App account does not automatically cancel a Google Play or Apple App Store subscription. Store subscriptions must be managed through the applicable store account.

10. Your Choices

You can choose not to create an account for functionality that is available without sign-in. You control which files you select and which external apps/services you choose when sharing or opening files.

11. Children

The App is a general-purpose file utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to operate the App and its account features.

12. International Processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the providers’ contractual, security, and legal safeguards.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.
