Account Deletion

Dosya Dönüştürücü / File Converter
Package: com.berke.dosyadonusturucu

You can request deletion of your Dosya Dönüştürücü / File Converter account and associated account data.

Delete from the App

Open the App and go to Settings → Account & Premium. Use the available account-deletion option to start deletion.

Request Deletion Outside the App

If you cannot access the App, send an account-deletion request to:

Email: YOUR_PRIVACY_EMAIL

Suggested subject: Delete my Dosya Dönüştürücü account

Send the request from the email address associated with your App account when possible. We may request reasonable verification before deleting account data in order to prevent unauthorized deletion.

What Is Deleted

Upon a valid deletion request, account-associated data under our control is deleted or anonymized as appropriate, including account records that are no longer required to operate or secure the service.

What May Be Retained

Limited information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud/abuse prevention, legal compliance, financial recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or enforcement of purchase and trial rules. Such retention is limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your App account does not cancel an active Google Play or Apple App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through the applicable store account.
